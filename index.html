Common questions

Where is my progress kept?

On your device, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server, so your coins, diamonds, best round, upgrades and unlocked balls live in the app itself.

I deleted the app and lost everything. Can you restore it?

I am afraid not. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing for me to look up — deleting the app removes the save with it. Moving to a new phone via an iCloud device backup usually carries it across; a fresh install does not.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. It plays exactly the same on a plane.

Closing the app mid-round lost the round.

That one is by design. Everything you have earned is saved, but the wall, the balls in the air and the boss on the board are not — a round is short, and storing all of it to survive an interruption was not worth what it would cost. You lose that round and nothing else.

What are the daily challenges, and when do they change?

Three every day — one easy, one medium, one hard — and they reset at midnight, your local time. They are drawn from the date itself, so everyone gets the same three and reloading will not reroll them.

How do I get more diamonds?

Two ways: best-round milestones, and the daily challenges. Diamonds open mystery boxes, and each box gives you a ball you do not already own.

Why does Leanberg get easier some rounds?

He drops his guard on his third and fifth rounds and takes far more damage. The background tells you: it beats faster and harder while he is open. Save a good board position for it.
