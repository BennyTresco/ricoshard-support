Privacy policy

RicoShard · last updated 22 August 2026

RicoShard collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. The game has no way to contact a server, because it has no network code beyond loading its own files from your device.

What the game stores

Your progress is saved on your device and stays there:

  • coins, diamonds and your best round
  • which upgrades you have bought and which balls you have unlocked
  • which milestones and daily challenges you have claimed
  • your music and sound-effect volume settings

That is the whole list. None of it identifies you, none of it leaves the device, and I cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email or any other detail.
  • It does not use analytics or crash reporting.
  • It does not show advertising or use an advertising identifier.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not include any third-party software that collects data.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, so there is nothing collected from a child either. It is rated for older players because a character in it drinks, not because of anything to do with data.

Purchases

This version of RicoShard has no in-app purchases. If that changes, the payment is handled entirely by Apple and I never see your card details — and this page will be updated before any such version is released.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game collects anything, this page will say so plainly, and the date at the top will change. I will not quietly widen it.
