Privacy policy

RicoShard · last updated November 2026 · applies from version 1.1

Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google, and those ads collect some data — how much is up to you. There is no account and no sign-in, and I never see who you are.

What the game stores on your device

  • coins, diamonds and your best round
  • the run you are in the middle of, so you can continue it
  • which upgrades you have bought and which balls you have unlocked
  • which milestones and daily challenges you have claimed
  • whether you have bought No Forced Ads
  • your music and sound-effect volume settings

None of this leaves your device, and I cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

RicoShard is free and pays for itself with ads served by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad when you leave a finished run, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for a second chance.

To show and measure those ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • the type of device, its operating system and language
  • your approximate location, worked out from your internet connection (never GPS)
  • which ads were shown and whether you tapped them, and basic diagnostics such as crashes in the ad software

Google uses this as described in its own policy: how Google uses information from apps that use its services. I receive only totals — how many ads were shown and what they earned — never anything about you personally.

Your choices

  • Tracking. The first time you play, iOS asks whether RicoShard may track you. If you say no, ads are still shown but are not based on your activity in other apps. You can change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland. You are asked for consent before any personal data is used for ads. You can change your answer any time with the PRIVACY button on the game's info page (the cog, then the info button).
  • Some US states. You can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data with the same PRIVACY button.
  • No Forced Ads. Buying it turns off the banner and the end-of-run ad for good. The second-chance ad stays available, but only ever plays if you choose to watch it.

Purchases

Diamonds and No Forced Ads are bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment entirely; I never see your name, card or Apple ID, only that a purchase was made.

Update check

When the game starts it reads one small file from this website to find out whether a newer version is available. No information about you or your progress is sent. Like any website, the host (GitHub) may briefly log the request.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email or any other detail.
  • It does not use analytics of its own.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • It does not sell anything about you. The data above is collected by Google for advertising, under the choices described above.

Children

RicoShard is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If what the game collects changes, this page will say so plainly, and the date at the top will change.
